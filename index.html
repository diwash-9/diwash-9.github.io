Bidhan विधान

Live

The Constitution of Nepal runs to 308 articles, and most people meet it as a PDF. What if studying it felt closer to a language app than to homework?

Bidhan organizes every article into structured lessons along a branching unlock path. Each lesson has two phases, reading first and a knowledge check second, with hearts, XP, streaks, leagues, and leaderboards around them. There is full text search and a role gated admin panel with an audited amendment trail.

Part 1: Art. 4 --> Art. 5 --> Art. 6
                \-> Art. 9 (unlocks after 4 + 5)
328 edges like this, not a straight line
Stack
React 18, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Query, FastAPI, PostgreSQL 17
Content
308 articles, 35 parts, 1,200+ quiz questions
Hosting
Vercel, Render, Neon, with CI on both ends

Matchday

Live, v1.0.0

Small tournaments tend to run on a spreadsheet, a group chat, and hope. Scores go missing, tables go stale, and nobody knows when they play next.

Matchday carries a tournament from registration to certificates: an animated group draw, a fixture console, derived points tables, knockout brackets with winner propagation, a certificate designer with bulk PDF export, and public pages per tournament that teams can follow without logging in.

Stack
Next.js 16, React 19, Prisma, Turso, Tailwind v4
Tests
85 Vitest tests across unit and action suites

Developer tools.

Utilities for the Omarchy Linux desktop.

omarchy-bsdate

Maintained

The bar clock speaks Gregorian, while daily life in Nepal runs on Bikram Sambat. This plugin puts both dates side by side and opens a full calendar on click, with a Roman/Devanagari toggle. All data is embedded, so it works offline.

QML, Bash, Quickshell

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/diwash-9/omarchy-bsdate --enable

Repository

omarchy-minimize (OmaVeil)

Maintained

Hyprland has no minimize action, only workspaces. This script treats a special workspace as a minimize stack: Super+H hides the focused window, Super+I picks from a native fuzzy list, Super+U restores the last one. Pure Bash, no daemon.

Bash, hyprctl, jq

Repository

omarchy-ytmp3

Maintained

Saving a lecture for offline listening meant ad-ridden converter sites. This takes a link from the clipboard, asks for format and folder through the launcher, and downloads with yt-dlp and ffmpeg, metadata included. It also works from the terminal and handles playlists.

Bash, yt-dlp, ffmpeg

yay -S omarchy-ytmp3

Repository

03. Teaching

Teaching.

Video lectures for CSIT, BCA, BIM, and engineering students through Digital Nomad Academy, plus study material work with HamroCSIT. Subjects include Numerical Methods, data structures and algorithms, operating systems, networks, DBMS, and cryptography.

Numerical Method in Nepali

26 videosDigital Nomad Academy

About: chapter by chapter Numerical Methods, from non-linear equations through interpolation, regression, differentiation, and integration, with worked examples and one-shot recaps.

Why it exists: the subject is compulsory and dense, and most references are in English. Teaching it in Nepali lowers the barrier for students seeing these methods the first time.

For: bachelor level CSIT, BCA, BIM, and engineering students taking Numerical Methods.

Open on YouTube

Complete EDA with Python: 25 Years of Premier League Data

10 videosDigital Nomad Academy

About: an end to end exploratory data analysis in Python on a real football dataset: understanding the data, cleaning it, descriptive statistics, uni/bi/multivariate analysis, correlation, feature engineering, and football insights.

Why it exists: EDA tutorials often use tiny toy datasets. Working through 25 years of messy match data shows the unglamorous middle of data work: cleaning, checking, and questioning.

For: Python learners and students meeting data analysis for the first time.

Open on YouTube

BSc.CSIT Semester-wise Breakdown

3 videosDigital Nomad Academy

About: walkthroughs of the first, second, and third semester CSIT syllabi with study resources for each.

Why it exists: new CSIT students often ask the same questions: what comes when, what matters, and where to study it. This answers those once, on video.

For: current and incoming BSc.CSIT students planning their semesters.
