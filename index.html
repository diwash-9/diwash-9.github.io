Bidhan विधान

Live

The Constitution of Nepal runs to 308 articles, and most people meet it as a PDF. What if studying it felt closer to a language app than to homework?

Bidhan organizes all 308 articles into lessons along a branching unlock path. Each lesson pairs reading with a quiz, with hearts, XP, streaks, and leaderboards. Includes full-text search and an admin panel.

Part 1: Art. 4 --> Art. 5 --> Art. 6
                \-> Art. 9 (unlocks after 4 + 5)
328 edges like this, not a straight line
Stack
React 18, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL 17
Content
308 articles, 35 parts, 1,200+ quiz questions

Matchday

Live, v1.0.0

Small tournaments tend to run on a spreadsheet, a group chat, and hope. Scores go missing, tables go stale, and nobody knows when they play next.

Matchday carries a tournament from registration to certificates: group draw, fixtures, points tables, knockout brackets, a certificate designer, and public pages that teams can follow without logging in.

Stack
Next.js 16, React 19, Prisma, Turso

Open the app Source is private for now.

Utilities for the Omarchy Linux desktop.

omarchy-bsdate

Maintained

Nepali date widget and calendar popup for the Omarchy bar. Shows Bikram Sambat alongside the English date, works offline, supports Roman and Devanagari.

QML, Bash, Quickshell

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/diwash-9/omarchy-bsdate --enable

Repository

omarchy-minimize (OmaVeil)

Maintained

Minimize and restore workflow for Hyprland. Super+H hides a window, Super+I finds it again, Super+U brings back the last one. Written in Bash, no daemon.

Bash, hyprctl, jq

Repository

omarchy-ytmp3

Maintained

YouTube to MP3 and MP4 downloader for Omarchy, with launcher and terminal use. Handles playlists and adds basic metadata. Available on the AUR.

Bash, yt-dlp, ffmpeg

yay -S omarchy-ytmp3
